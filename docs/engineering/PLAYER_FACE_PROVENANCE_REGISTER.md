# Player Face Provenance & Licensing Register
**PES 6 Legacy Remaster: Intellectual Property, Licensing & Reference Provenance**

## 1. Overview & Rights Management Policy

PES 6 Legacy Remaster adheres to strict asset provenance and licensing standards. Every reference photograph utilized in Stage 18 has been vetted against Wikimedia Commons licensing metadata. Commercial-use restrictions, non-derivative restrictions, and unverified editorial images have been systematically excluded.

All derivative face textures generated by our pipeline are synthesized transformations conforming to PES 6's original 3D mesh UV parameterization.
