## 1. Visual Comparison Overview

The visual enhancements in the PES 6 Legacy Remaster target high-impact elements that define the match experience:
1. **Aspect Ratio & Field of View:** Transitioning from stretched 4:3 graphics to native 16:9 widescreen projection with natural player proportions.
2. **Pitch Turf Clarity:** Replacing low-resolution tiling textures with 2048x1024 / 4K multi-frequency turf with natural mower striping.
3. **Color Grading & Tonemapping:** Introducing filmic S-curve contrast and +15% grass vibrance to eliminate washed-out 2006 palette limitations.
4. **Broadcast Scoreboard Bug:** Introducing modern television broadcast score, clock, and stoppage time indicators.
5. **Original Player Likenesses:** 100% original Konami 2006 face meshes, textures, and palettes cleanly preserved.
